Everything unlocked

What every button was meant to do

Tested on the MX Master 3 and 2S, designed to adapt to other HID++ Logitech devices (MX Master 3S, MX Anywhere, MX Vertical) via feature detection.

⌘ Button remapping

Per-button actions: keyboard shortcuts, system actions like Mission Control, synthetic clicks or raw gesture directions.

⚡ SmartShift

Ratchet, free-spin or automatic wheel mode with sensitivity control — right from the Buttons tab.

🎯 DPI cycling & swap

Step through 1000 / 1600 / 2000 / 4000 DPI from any button, and swap left/right physically at the device level.

↔️ Thumb wheel

Independent left/right actions for the horizontal thumb wheel, including shortcuts and volume.

👋 Gestures

Raw XY movement classified into directions — bind swipe gestures to Spaces, desktops or anything else.

🏃 Run Shortcut

Trigger any Apple Shortcut from a mouse button. Launch scripts, apps and workflows without touching the keyboard.

Feel

Trackpad glide,
mouse wheel

RatTamer reads hi-res wheel deltas over HID++ and re-emits them as continuous pixel scrolling — momentum, glide and bounce tuned to feel like a trackpad.

  • ◦ One slider: Discreta, Média or Fluida presets
  • ◦ Advanced panel with momentum, glide, feed and bounce tuning
  • ◦ Live tuning playground included (RatTest)
