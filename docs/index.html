Your MX Master,

RaTamer speaks HID++ 2.0 directly to your Logitech mouse — every button remappable, ratchet / free-spin / SmartShift, DPI cycling and trackpad-style smooth scrolling. Quit the app and the mouse returns to stock.

Features

Everything the official app does.
Minus the account.

Button remapping

Keyboard shortcuts, system actions, synthetic clicks, gesture directions or DPI cycling — per button.

Wheel modes

Ratchet, free-spin or SmartShift automatic, with a sensitivity slider.

Thumb wheel

Separate left and right actions, independent of the main wheel.

Smooth scrolling

Hi-res wheel deltas re-emitted as pixel scroll. Nine presets from Native to Fluid; the slider snaps onto each one.

Advanced tuning

Momentum decay, glide fraction, feed timing, bounce filter, direction threshold — all live.

Scroll thermograph

A separate window plotting raw versus smoothed deltas while you scroll.

Feel it

The slider is magnetic

Drag it. Just like in the app, it snaps onto a preset when you pass one — six of them live between 80 and 90, so you stop guessing.

Requirements

Will it run?

A regular app in your Applications folder — no helper daemons, no account. Accessibility permission is required to post events. Use the Unifying/Bolt receiver for full HID++ support; some mice don't expose HID++ over Bluetooth.
